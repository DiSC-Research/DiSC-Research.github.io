Director

Hexu Liu, Ph.D.

Associate Professor · Civil and Construction Engineering · Western Michigan University

Dr. Liu leads research on AI-enabled construction systems, industrialized construction, infrastructure digital twins, and construction market intelligence. His work connects BIM, data-driven methods, and lifecycle analytics to practical decisions across building and transportation systems.

His research is supported by NSF and state transportation and safety agencies, and appears in Automation in Construction, Advanced Engineering Informatics, and leading ASCE journals.
