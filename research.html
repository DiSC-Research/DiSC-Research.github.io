Research agenda

Three research thrusts connected by shared methods.

Each thrust moves from a domain problem to a computational method, an applied system, and a validated outcome.

01

AI-Enabled Industrialized Construction

Reliable LLM-driven BIM automation, generative design, semantic BIM, DfMA, modular construction, and off-site production planning.

AI-BIM automation
02

Infrastructure Lifecycle Intelligence

Bridge condition assessment, digital twins, deterioration models, computer vision, structural health monitoring, predictive maintenance, and life-cycle cost.

Digital twins + data fusion
03

Construction Market Intelligence

Highway construction cost indices, price-volatility forecasting, bidder behavior, procurement analysis, work-zone user costs, and DOT decision support.

Human-AI decision support
