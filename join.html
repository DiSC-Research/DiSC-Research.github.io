Graduate research opportunities

Bring engineering judgment and computation together.

DiSC Lab welcomes inquiries from students interested in AI-enabled construction, digital infrastructure systems, and intelligent engineering applications.

Application timelineSpring admission: September 15, 2026Fall admission: March 15, 2027

Prospective students should review the research areas and application materials before contacting the lab.

Research opportunities

Problems we are working on.

Projects span building, transportation, public safety, and construction education systems.

01

AI-enabled construction automation

LLM-driven BIM workflows, generative design, semantic BIM, and reliable human-AI collaboration.

02

Digital twins for infrastructure

Condition assessment, data fusion, predictive maintenance, and lifecycle decisions for bridges and facilities.

03

Construction cost intelligence

Highway cost indices, forecasting, bidding behavior, lifecycle cost, and agency decision support.

04

Industrialized construction

DfMA, modular and off-site production, BIM-based planning, and construction automation.

Desired background

What helps you succeed here.

  • Civil, construction, computer science, or a related engineering background
  • Programming experience in Python, C#, or a similar language
  • Experience with BIM, AI, machine learning, or data analytics
  • Strong research motivation and comfort with interdisciplinary work

What to send

A concise application package.

  • Curriculum vitae
  • Unofficial transcript
  • Brief statement of research interests and fit with DiSC Lab
  • Optional evidence such as papers, code, BIM models, or project work

Why DiSC Lab

Research with technical depth and public relevance.

Interdisciplinary work bridging civil engineering and computer science.

Strong publication and graduate research support.

Engagement with real infrastructure projects supported by NSF and public agencies.

How to apply

Introduce your research direction, not only your résumé.

Send your materials and briefly explain which DiSC Lab research thrust you hope to contribute to.
