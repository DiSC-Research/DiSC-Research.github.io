Research snapshot · March 2026

Research identity

Turning fragmented built-environment data into validated decisions.

Construction and infrastructure systems generate disconnected information across design models, field operations, agency records, cost histories, maintenance logs, and asset-management systems.

DiSC Lab develops domain-grounded AI, BIM, and digital-twin systems that connect those sources to practical decisions for construction delivery, infrastructure management, and public agencies.

Core research thrusts

Three systems. One decision-intelligence agenda.
